# Synthetic Retail Banking Dataset Methodology

This dataset is synthetic and does not represent ICICI Bank or real customers.

It was generated from unequal, overlapping behavioral populations with different drivers: financial capacity, engagement, credit reliance, digital preference and savings preference. Different banking variables receive different strengths of influence and additional noise.

The hidden generation labels are not included in the final CSV. K-Means therefore works only from observable customer behavior.

Gender, City Tier and Occupation are retained for descriptive profiling only and are not used as primary clustering inputs.

Because the data is synthetic, it cannot prove real-world customer behavior, product demand, demographic fairness or ICICI-specific outcomes.
