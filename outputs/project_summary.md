# Final Project Summary

## Objective
Customer Segmentation & Cross-Sell Analytics for Retail Banking.

## Dataset
100,000 synthetic retail-banking customer records. Not ICICI customer data.

## Workflow
Cleaning → EDA → SQL business analysis → feature engineering → weighted K-Means → segment profiling → Power BI.

## Business dimensions
- Financial Capacity: 35%
- Engagement: 30%
- Credit Dependence: 20%
- Digital Behavior: 15%

## Final segments
- Prime Engaged
- Digital Active
- Credit Active
- Affluent Under-Engaged

1. Customer Overview
2. Segment Deep Dive
3. Business Opportunities & Insights
